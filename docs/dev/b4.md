* `batch_size` = **384**

$$98,304 \times 2 = 196,608 \text{ token assignments.}$$

### 2. The Thesis Goldmine: The "Over-Parameterized Routing Regime"
If you scale to $E = 128$ experts, you create a very rare and publishable scenario:
* **The Dimension Limit:** Your model's `d_model` is **128**. If `num_experts` is also **128**, your routing gate projection layer ($nn.Linear(128, 128)$) is mapping a 128-dimensional hidden representation to 128 independent routing scores.
* In standard large-scale MoEs (like Mixtral or DeepSeek), the ratio of $E$ to $d\_model$ is tiny (e.g. $E=64$ for $d\_model=4096$). 
* By setting $E \ge d\_model$, you are testing the **over-parameterized expert regime** at a tiny compute cost. 

#### Why `reflected` will dominate here:

1. **Standard `top2`** will likely suffer from severe **routing churn** (token routing assignments changing wildly and chaotically step-by-step), struggling to build true expert specialization.
2. **`reflected_top2`** will leverage its load reflection pressure to actively force the router to make extremely confident, sharp, low-entropy choices. The adversarial pressure dynamics will act as a stabilizing anchor, preventing routing chaos.
